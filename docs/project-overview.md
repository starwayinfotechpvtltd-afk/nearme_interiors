# Project Overview: Near Me Interiors

## Executive Summary
Near Me Interiors is a world-class, premium, conversion-focused digital marketing agency specifically designed for elite interior designers and architects. This platform acts as the agency's primary digital flagship, communicating high trust, deep expertise, and measurable growth.

## Core Value Proposition
We help luxury interior design studios sign their next 7-figure project using data-driven digital marketing. By marrying luxury design sensibilities with rigorous growth marketing systems (SEO, Paid Acquisition, Web Optimization), we solve the high-end lead-acquisition problem.

## High-Level Goals
- Establish absolute authority in the interior design marketing niche.
- Deliver an interface that feels like a luxury publication + high-converting SaaS dashboard.
- Optimize for high-tier conversions: Strategy Call Bookings, Guide Downloads, and Case Study interactions.
