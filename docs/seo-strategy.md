# SEO Strategy: Near Me Interiors

## On-Page Architecture
- **Semantic Structure:** 1x `<h1>` tag per page, logical nesting of heading levels (`<h2>`, `<h3>`).
- **Schema Markups:**
  - Organization Schema on home page.
  - Local Business & Service Schema on dynamic location and service pages.
  - FAQ Rich Snippets using JSON-LD for accordions.
  - Review / Testimonial Schema for credibility.

## Keyword Targets
- Primary: "Interior Design Marketing Agency", "SEO for Interior Designers", "Google Ads for Interior Designers"
- Localized: "Interior Design Marketing Agency Mumbai", "Delhi Interior Design Marketing", etc.
