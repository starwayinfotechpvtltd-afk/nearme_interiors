# Branding Guidelines: Near Me Interiors

## Brand Identity & Persona
Near Me Interiors represents the intersection of structural design principles and marketing velocity. We are sophisticated, analytical, elegant, and results-oriented.

## Typography
- **Heading Font:** Playfair Display or Outfit (Luxury, modern architectural serif/sans).
- **Body Font:** Inter or Roboto (Highly readable, clean, engineered feel).

## Brand Voice & Copywriting
- **Style:** Authoritative, sophisticated, yet direct. We speak in numbers and outcomes, avoiding fluffy buzzwords.
- **Keywords:** Elevation, precision, architectural growth, revenue design, visibility, ROI, luxury acquisition.
